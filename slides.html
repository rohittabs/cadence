The thing nobody tells beginners

20 min

a day beats three hours on Sunday.

Fingers learn by repetition spread over time. One long session cannot buy back a week of silence.

But one missed day is never the problem

Everybody skips a day. That is normal life.

What actually ends a routine is the quiet week after the missed day, when nothing reminds you that you stopped.

So Cadence 3.0 notices.

It only tells you what is true

Ask it which day you are most consistent, how much you have played all time, what your mood has been, whether you are on track for your weekly goal. It works all of it out on your phone.

Ask it how to play a Bm chord and it will tell you it cannot help with that.

That is what your teacher is for.
